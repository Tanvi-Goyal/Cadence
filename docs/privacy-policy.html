MIND[SET] — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6th September 2026 · Android application com.mindset.athlete

MIND[SET] is a training tracker for HYROX athletes. It is built to work offline: your training data lives on your device, not on a server we operate. This policy explains the limited information the app does handle, and who else it reaches.

1. Data stored only on your device

The app records your workouts, sessions, exercises, race results and station splits, together with the profile you enter during onboarding — race division, gender and preferred weight unit. All of it is written to a database in the app's private storage on your phone.

We never receive this data. MIND[SET] has no account system and no backend server. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

2. Crash diagnostics

The app uses Google Firebase Crashlytics so that crashes can be found and fixed. When the app crashes, Crashlytics sends a report containing:

What Why
Stack trace of the crash Identify the faulty code
Device model, operating system version, app version Reproduce the conditions
A randomly generated installation identifier Group repeated crashes from one install
Device state at the time (memory, storage, orientation) Diagnose the cause

This is diagnostic data. It is not linked to your name or to any account, it is not used for advertising, and it is never sold. It is processed by Google on our behalf under the Firebase Privacy and Security policy. Crash reports are retained by Firebase for a limited period and then deleted automatically.

3. Backup to your own Google Drive

If Android Backup is enabled on your device, Android copies your MIND[SET] training database and preferences to your Google Drive account, so a new phone can restore them. This is a feature of Android, not of us: the backup sits in your Google account, and the developer has no access to it and cannot read it.

You can turn this off in Android Settings → Google → Backup, and you can delete existing backups from your Google account.

4. Content loaded from other sites

Exercise illustrations and muscle diagrams are fetched on demand from wger.de and from raw.githubusercontent.com. As with any request to a website, those services necessarily see your device's IP address and may log it under their own policies. The app sends them no identifier, no account and no training data — only a request for an image.

5. Permissions the app requests

Permission Used for
INTERNET Downloading exercise images and sending crash reports
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Showing the live race timer notification
FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE Keeping the race clock running accurately while the app is in the background or the screen is off

The app requests no access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, body sensors, health data, files or the phone's advertising identifier.

6. What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in or personal profile stored anywhere but your device.
  • No advertising, ad networks, or advertising identifiers.
  • No behavioural or usage analytics.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal data with third parties.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.

7. Children

MIND[SET] is intended for adult athletes and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your choices and deleting your data

  • All training data: uninstall the app; it is deleted with it.
  • Drive backups: delete them from your Google account's backup settings.
  • Crash reports: contact us at the address below and we will request deletion of reports associated with your installation identifier.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently — for example when account sign-in or server sync is added — this page will be updated and the date at the top changed before that version ships.
